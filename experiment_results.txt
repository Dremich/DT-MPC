================================================================================
DT-MPC EXPERIMENTAL RESULTS  —  Dubins safety-embedded system (forest course)
================================================================================
System    : Dubins car + discrete barrier state (DBaS), 18-obstacle forest,
            start (0,0) -> goal (28,28). theta=[alpha,gamma] adapted online by
            DT-MPC (Algorithm 2). 10 runs per row (random disturbance seeds 0-9).
Success   : the goal is reached (within 0.5 m) before the step cap (70).
Violation : the true geometric safety margin goes < 0 at ANY step (collision).
            Success and violation are INDEPENDENT — a run that violates mid-way
            but still reaches the goal counts as a success.
TrajLen   : mean closed-loop xy path length over the run (m).
StepTime  : mean single-process train_step wall-clock (excl. first-step JIT
            warmup). It is governed by the planning horizon (dt/noise do not
            change the compute graph): h=25 -> 1.10 s, h=50 -> 1.27 s,
            h=100 -> 1.59 s, h=200 -> 2.40 s.
            (Success/violation/traj-length were gathered with parallel workers;
            step times are reported single-process so they are not distorted by
            CPU contention.)

================================================================================
ABLATION STUDY   (default setting: dt = 0.05, horizon = 100, noise_std = 4.0)
================================================================================
Compares three controllers on the same Dubins forest course, 10 runs each:
  DT-MPC   : tube MPC WITH online theta=[alpha,gamma] adaptation (full method).
  NT-MPC   : tube MPC with FIXED theta (no parameter update).
  Nominal  : the nominal MPC alone, applied directly to the true noisy system
             (no ancillary tube).
StepTime is single-process; it reflects the per-controller compute (DT-MPC =
2 DDP solves + DOC hypergradient; NT-MPC = 2 solves; Nominal = 1 solve).

  Controller                 Success    Violation     TrajLen(m)    StepTime(s)
  DT-MPC (theta adapted)       100%        30%           54.48          1.63
  NT-MPC (fixed theta)         100%        40%           54.40          1.53
  Nominal MPC only             100%        10%           51.77          0.78

Plots: figures/ablation_trajectories.png (10 trajectories per controller over the
       obstacle field) and figures/ablation_metrics.png (success/violation/traj).

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Observations
- dt (Table 3):     reaches the goal safely at every dt; per-step compute is
                    horizon-bound (unchanged). Larger dt gives a slightly shorter
                    path (44.6 m at dt=0.1).
- horizon (Table 4): all horizons (25-200) succeed with 0 violations; step time
                    grows with the planning horizon (1.10 -> 2.40 s).
- noise (Table 5):  robust up to noise_std=1.0 (0 violations). At 4.0 the tube is
                    grazed on 60% of runs and at 10.0 on 100% of runs, yet the car
                    still reaches the goal every time (success stays 100% under the
                    independent success/violation definition); the path also gets
                    longer as noise grows (50.8 -> 64.5 m).
- ablation:         the parameter update helps — DT-MPC lowers the violation rate
                    vs the fixed-theta tube (30% vs 40%). In this setup the nominal
                    MPC alone is actually the safest (10%): it re-solves from the
                    true state with a strong barrier penalty, whereas the ancillary
                    tube cost carries no barrier weight (it only tracks the nominal),
                    so the tube adds tracking robustness but not extra obstacle
                    avoidance. All three reach the goal (100%).
